- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (insight by user) Core job-to-be-done: help people ideate and experiment with their outfits
- (idea by coach) Remix button: swap one item in a saved Fit for a random compatible alternative from wardrobe, turning a static Fit into a jumping-off point
- (direction by user) Also want: monetization angle, addictive retention loop, and plans to add an AI agent to help plan outfits
- (decision) Verdict for MVP retention priority: (1) wear-count/cost-per-wear = cheap high-value hook, ship now; (2) AI outfit-planning agent = highest addiction/monetization potential but post-MVP, needs paid gate; (3) custom categories = low retention value, organization feature not an engagement hook, build later for power users
- (idea by coach) Wardrobe utilization stat: % of wardrobe worn in last 30/90 days, surface cold-spot items unworn 60+ days (guilt/pride loop, reuses existing wear data)
- (idea by coach) Weekly/monthly recap card (Spotify-Wrapped style): most-worn item, favorite color, longest streak - shareable, gives a reason to open app on a cadence
- (idea by coach) Streak milestone rewards: unlock a canvas theme/template at 7/30/100-day streaks, good spot for a premium-trial nudge
- (idea by coach) Reminder notifications (plan tomorrow's Fit tonight): strongest single retention lever available, but currently conflicts with FR34's no-reminders non-goal - flagged for reconsideration post-MVP, not scoped in yet
- (insight) Synthesis: core JTBD is 'ideate and experiment with outfits.' MVP-safe engagement set = wear-count/cost-per-wear, wardrobe utilization stat, weekly/monthly recap card, streak milestone rewards - all reuse existing fit_wears data and existing streak mechanic. Bigger levers (AI planning agent, reminder notifications, lightweight social, affiliate prompts) are real but conflict with current Phase 1 non-goals or need a paid gate - deferred to a deliberate post-MVP decision, not silently scoped in.
